Many of you probably remember when you were getting
into this business, you tried a bunch of stuff,
most of it didn't work and then something started to work.
This is the risk of new.
And I think this is one
of the most misjudged risks in business.
Like the time you do new is when you start.
And basically after that,
everything is almost more or better.
And typically more with a feedback loop just becomes better.
And the reason that we want to go after more
is because more is the highest risk adjusted move
that you can do.
