# Hook Writing Guide for Clips

Hooks are the single most important element. They determine scroll-past vs watch.

## The 5 Hook Patterns

### 1. Problem Statement (highest converter)
Lead with the pain. Don't ease into it.

**Examples:**
- "Stripe just killed your store. What now?"
- "Your payment processor just shut you down. No warning."
- "Frozen account. Zero revenue. 48 hours to recover."

**When to use:** Clips where the speaker describes a catastrophic event.

### 2. Hidden Truth (curiosity gap)
Promise information they don't have.

**Examples:**
- "The hidden clause Stripe doesn't want you to see."
- "90% of merchants don't know this about reserves."
- "The real reason your account got frozen."

**When to use:** Clips that expose mechanisms, policies, or industry secrets.

### 3. The Statistic (shock value)
Numbers hit harder than words.

**Examples:**
- "$120K tied up in reserves. 90 days. No warning."
- "2.9% is marketing. The effective rate is 4.7%."
- "8,000 merchants frozen last month alone."

**When to use:** Clips with quantifiable claims — dollar amounts, percentages, timeframes.

### 4. The Question (engagement bait)
Make them answer internally.

**Examples:**
- "Why does this keep happening to successful businesses?"
- "What happens when your biggest win triggers a shutdown?"
- "How long can you survive without access to your money?"

**When to use:** Clips that describe patterns or recurring problems.

### 5. The Contrast (paradox)
Success and failure in the same sentence.

**Examples:**
- "Your biggest win can trigger a shutdown."
- "Start first. Evaluate after. Your money at risk."
- "They move fast because they don't underwrite deeply."

**When to use:** Clips that show the gap between expectation and reality.

## Platform-Specific Hook Rules

| Platform | Max Length | Tone | Style |
|----------|-----------|------|-------|
| TikTok | 6-8 words | Urgent, raw, direct | Pattern 1 or 5 |
| Instagram Reels | 8-10 words | Clean, authoritative | Pattern 2 or 3 |
| YouTube Shorts | 10-12 words | Educational, curious | Pattern 4 or 2 |
| LinkedIn | 12-15 words | Professional, insight | Pattern 3 or 2 |
| X/Twitter | 10-12 words | Sharp, punchy | Pattern 1 or 5 |
| Facebook | 10-12 words | Conversational | Pattern 4 or 1 |

## Hook → CTA Connection

Every hook should logically lead to the CTA. The hook creates the problem; the CTA solves it.

| Hook Pattern | Matching CTA |
|-------------|--------------|
| Problem Statement | "Start Free →" (immediate relief) |
| Hidden Truth | "See how it works →" (learn more) |
| Statistic | "Protect your revenue →" (take action) |
| Question | "Find out →" (curiosity resolution) |
| Contrast | "Switch to your-saas →" (choose the better option) |

## Anti-Patterns (What NOT to Do)

1. **Don't be vague:** "Payment processing is hard" → too generic
2. **Don't hide the lede:** "In this video we discuss..." → boring
3. **Don't use jargon:** "Aggregator model underwriting" → confusing
4. **Don't promise what the clip doesn't deliver:** Hook about crypto, clip about reserves → bait and switch
5. **Don't use ALL CAPS for entire hook:** Looks like spam, not content
