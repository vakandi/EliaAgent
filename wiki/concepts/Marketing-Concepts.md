## 📋 Key Principles

### Acquisition Economics
- Know your [[wiki/concepts/CAC|CAC]] (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- Maximize [[wiki/concepts/LTV|LTV]] (Lifetime Value)
- LTV:CAC ratio should exceed 3:1

### Offer Structure
- Front-end offer (initial sale)
- Backend offer (upsells, cross-sells)
- Subscription model for recurring revenue

### Traffic Sources
1. **Organic** — Content, SEO, social
2. **Paid** — Ads (Snapchat, TikTok, Meta)
3. **Referral** — Word of mouth
