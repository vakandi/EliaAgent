# Concept: Hypertext

## Definition

Hypertext is text displayed on a computer display or other electronic devices with references (links) to other text that the reader can immediately access. The term was coined by Ted Nelson in 1963.

## History

- **1963**: Ted Nelson coins the term "hypertext"
- **1989**: Tim Berners-Lee proposes the World Wide Web at CERN
- **1991**: First website goes live
- **Today**: Foundation of all web content

## Key Figures

- **Ted Nelson** - Coined "hypertext", project Xanadu
- **Tim Berners-Lee** - Invented World Wide Web

## Connection to Memex

Hypertext is the digital realization of Vannevar Bush's Memex vision:
- Associative linking between documents
- Personal trails through knowledge
- Direct connections between related items
