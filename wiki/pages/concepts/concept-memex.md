# Concept: Memex

## Definition

Vannevar Bush's conceptual machine for associatively linking human knowledge, described in "As We May Think" (1945). The precursor to hypertext and the modern web.

## Core Idea

> "A device in which an individual stores all his books, records, and communications, and which is mechanized so that it may be consulted with exceeding speed and flexibility."

## Key Features

1. **Associative Trails**: Links between related items, mimicking human thought
2. **Personal Library**: All of one's knowledge in one place
3. **Speed & Flexibility**: Instant retrieval and cross-referencing
4. **Mechanized**: Automation to handle the bookkeeping

## The Unresolved Problem

Bush identified that the tedious part of knowledge management is maintenance:
- Updating cross-references
- Keeping summaries current
- Tracking contradictions
- Filing new information

He couldn't solve who does this maintenance work.

## The LLM Solution

> "The part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The LLM handles that."

The LLM Wiki pattern is the modern realization of Bush's Memex vision.
