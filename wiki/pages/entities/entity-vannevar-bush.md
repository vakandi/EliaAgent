# Entity: Vannevar Bush

## Definition

American engineer, scientist, and administrator (1890-1974) who conceptualized the Memex and influenced early computing and information science.

## Key Attributes
- **Born/Died**: March 9, 1890 – June 30, 1974
- **Role**: Director of Office of Scientific Research and Development (WWII)
- **Known For**: Memex concept, differential analyzer (analog computer)
- **Famous Work**: "As We May Think" (The Atlantic, 1945)

## Contributions

### The Memex
In "As We May Think," Bush described a desk-like machine with:
- Microfilm storage for books and records
- Associative linking between items
- Speed and flexibility of retrieval

### Legacy
- Influenced Ted Nelson (Hypertext)
- Influenced Tim Berners-Lee (World Wide Web)
- Vision finally realized with LLM Wiki pattern

## Connection to LLM Wiki

Bush's vision was:
> "Private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves."

The LLM solves the maintenance problem he couldn't.
