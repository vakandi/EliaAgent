# Entity: Sam Altman

## Definition

Samuel Harris Altman (born 1985) is an American entrepreneur, programmer, and investor. He is CEO of OpenAI and former president of Y Combinator.

## Key Attributes

- **Born**: April 22, 1985
- **Role**: CEO of OpenAI (since 2019)
- **Previous**: President of Y Combinator (2014-2019)
- **Notable**: Led OpenAI's transition from non-profit to "capped profit"

## Career Highlights

### Y Combinator
- Became president of the startup accelerator at age 29
- Oversaw massive growth of the program
- Led investments in Airbnb, Stripe, Reddit, etc.

### OpenAI
- Joined as co-chairman in 2018
- Became CEO in 2019
- Led development of GPT-3, GPT-4, ChatGPT
- Navigated company through AI safety debates
