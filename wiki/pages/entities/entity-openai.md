# Entity: OpenAI

## Definition

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research company founded in 2015, known for creating GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) models and ChatGPT.

- **Headquarters**: San Francisco, California

- **Notable Products**: GPT-4, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Sora
