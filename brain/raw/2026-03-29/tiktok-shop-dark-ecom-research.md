## 🔍 [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] SHOP "NEW" vs "USED" DETECTION

### What We Found

| Detection Aspect | Status | Details |
|-----------------|--------|---------|
| **[[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#products|Product]] Condition** | ⚠️ Partial | [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] Shop HAS condition fields (New/Used) |
| **Automatic Detection** | ❌ Unclear | No public evidence of automatic "new vs used" [[../../wiki/concepts/AI-Automation|AI]] detection |
| **[[../../wiki/concepts/Pricing|Price]] + Condition Combo** | ⚠️ Risk | Selling "new" items at used prices = RED FLAG |
| **Authentication** | ✅ Active | LegitApp checks authenticity, not condition |

### [[../../wiki/concepts/File-Management|Key]] Insight: "New" vs "Used" Listing

**How [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] Shop handles condition:**
- Sellers SELECT condition (New, Used, Refurbished)
- [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] does NOT automatically detect if listing is inaccurate
- HOWEVER: If buyer requests authentication and item is "new" but actually counterfeit/used = problem
- [[../../wiki/concepts/Pricing|Price]] anomaly detection EXISTS but is mainly for pricing fraud, not condition

### The "New" Status Risk

| Scenario | Risk Level | Reason |
|----------|------------|--------|
| Listing "new" but counterfeit | 🔴 HIGH | LegitApp will fail authentication |
| Listing "new" but actually used | 🟡 MEDIUM | Buyer complaint, not automatic ban |
| Listing "used" as "new" | 🟡 MEDIUM | Buyer dispute |
| Generic "new" (no brand) | 🟢 LOW | Harder to [[../../wiki/concepts/Prompt-Engineering|VERIFY]]/authenticate |

### What Triggers Bans (Detailed)

1. **Trademark/Brand Infringement** - HIGH RISK 🔴
   - Using brand names ([[../../wiki/concepts/Luxury-Brands#chanel|Chanel]], [[../../wiki/concepts/Luxury-Brands#gucci|Gucci]], [[../../wiki/concepts/Luxury-Brands#louis-vuitton|LV]], [[../../wiki/concepts/Luxury-Brands#dior|Dior]]) in titles/descriptions
   - "High copy", "replica", "AAA quality", "super perfect" keywords
   - [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#products|Product]] [[../../wiki/skills/Higgsfield-Video|Images]] showing brand logos clearly
   - [[../../wiki/concepts/AI-Automation|AI]] image detection can identify brand logos automatically

2. **Authentication Requests** - HIGH RISK 🔴
   - Buyers can [[../../wiki/concepts/API-Integration|Request]] authentication for luxury items
   - LegitApp verifies authenticity (partnership [[../../wiki/topics/Infrastructure-Timeline|Since]] 2025)
   - Failed verification = automatic refund + ban risk + potential legal action

3. **[[../../wiki/concepts/Pricing|Price]] Anomalies** - MEDIUM RISK 🟡
   - Too cheap = suspicious (authenticity questions flagged)
   - Too expensive = no [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#revenue|Sales]], poor conversion
   - [[../../wiki/concepts/AI-Automation|AI]] may flag unrealistic pricing vs market average
   - **NEW (March 2026)**: Fictitious listings rules enforced

4. **Seller Reputation** - CRITICAL 🔴
   - New accounts = higher scrutiny (700K+ sellers removed in 2025)
   - Low ratings = faster ban
   - [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#account|Account]] age matters significantly
   - [[../../wiki/[[../../wiki/skills/Index|SKILLS]]/Higgsfield-Video|Video]] [[../../wiki/concepts/Marketing-Concepts|Content]] quality affects trust score

5. **[[../../wiki/concepts/Marketing-Concepts|Content]]/[[../../wiki/[[../../wiki/skills/Index|SKILLS]]/Higgsfield-Video|Video]] Detection** - MEDIUM RISK 🟡
   - [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]]'s [[../../wiki/concepts/AI-Automation|AI]] analyzes [[../../wiki/[[../../wiki/skills/Index|SKILLS]]/Higgsfield-Video|Video]] [[../../wiki/concepts/Marketing-Concepts|Content]]
   - [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#products|Product]] in [[../../wiki/[[../../wiki/skills/Index|SKILLS]]/Higgsfield-Video|Video]] must match listing
   - Brand logos visible in [[../../wiki/[[../../wiki/skills/Index|SKILLS]]/Higgsfield-Video|Video]] = potential infringement [[../../wiki/docs/Sessions|Report]]
   - Music/sound can trigger [[../../wiki/concepts/Marketing-Concepts|Content]] ID claims

## 📋 RECOMMENDATION FOR [[../../wiki/people/Wael|Wael]] - ANSWERING YOUR QUESTIONS

### Question 1: "Will [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] bots stop us based on [[../../wiki/concepts/Pricing|Price]]?"

**Answer: PARTIAL RISK** 🟡
- [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] does have [[../../wiki/concepts/Pricing|Price]] anomaly detection
- If you sell [[../../wiki/concepts/Luxury-Brands#chanel|Chanel]] bags at €50 (retail €5000), it's an instant red flag
- Mid-[[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#sizing|Range]] pricing (30-50% of retail) looks more legitimate
- **Recommendation**: Use realistic pricing, not "too good to be true"

### Question 2: "Will [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] bots detect if items are 'new' vs 'used'?"

**Answer: LIMITED DETECTION** 🟡
- [[../../wiki/businesses/TikTok-YouTube-Auto|TikTok]] does NOT automatically detect "new vs used" in [[../../wiki/skills/Higgsfield-Video|Images]]
- HOWEVER: If buyer requests authentication and it's fake = instant ban
- Listing "new" but at used prices = potential review trigger
- **Recommendation**: Be consistent. Either sell as "used/vintage" OR as "new" with proper pricing

### Question 3: "Can we sell products that look like brand X without [[../../wiki/systems/Jira-Tickets-Index|Issues]]?"

**Answer: HIGH RISK** 🔴
- NO brand names in ANY text (titles, descriptions, tags)
- NO logos in [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe#products|Product]] [[../../wiki/skills/Higgsfield-Video|Images]] or videos
- NO "style", "inspired by", "type", "like" references
- [[../../wiki/concepts/AI-Automation|AI]] can detect logos in [[../../wiki/skills/Higgsfield-Video|Images]] automatically
- **Recommendation**: Sell generic products only, or private label
