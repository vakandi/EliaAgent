# Confusion About Product Name vs Damage

> [!links]+ Related
> [[../../wiki/businesses/Bene2Luxe|Bene2Luxe]] · [[../../wiki/businesses/B2LUXE-BUSINESS|B2LUXE BUSINESS]] · [[../../wiki/people/Wael|Wael]] · [[../../wiki/people/Ali|Ali]] · [[../../wiki/people/Rida|Rida]] · [[../../wiki/channels/WhatsApp-B2LUXE|WhatsApp B2LUXE]] · [[../../wiki/channels/Telegram|Telegram]] · [[../analysis/analysis-2026-04-09-10|Apr 9-10 Analysis]] · [[../../wiki/topics/Bene2Luxe-Timeline|Bene2Luxe Timeline]]

## What Happened

Misinterpreted "Blouson LV Brûlé" as a damaged item when "Brûlé" is actually the product name/designation.

This happened during [[../../wiki/businesses/B2LUXE-BUSINESS|B2LUXE BUSINESS]] [[../../wiki/channels/WhatsApp-B2LUXE|WhatsApp]] coordination between [[../../wiki/people/Ali|Ali]] and [[../../wiki/people/Rida|Rida]].

## The Confusion

| Message | Interpretation | Actual Meaning |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| "Blouson LV Brûlé" | Damaged blouson (burned) | Louis Vuitton Brûlé (colorway name) |
| [[../../wiki/people/Ali|Ali]]'s message | Problem with product | New product to list |
| Action taken | Investigate damage | Add to Shopify |

[[../../wiki/people/Wael|Wael]] clarified via [[../../wiki/channels/Telegram|Telegram]]: "Brûlé" is a color/design name, not a damage report.

## Root Cause Analysis

| Cause | Why It Happened |
|-------|----------------|
| **Language context** | "Brûlé" = burned in French |
| **Assumed negative** | Thought it meant damage |
| **Didn't ask first** | Should have clarified with [[../../wiki/people/Ali\|Ali]] |
| **No product catalog** | Didn't know naming conventions |
