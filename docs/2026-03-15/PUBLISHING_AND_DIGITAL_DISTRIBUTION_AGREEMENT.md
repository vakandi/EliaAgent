# PUBLISHING AND DIGITAL DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Publishing and Digital Distribution Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of last signature below (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**PUBLISHER:**
COFIBOU DISTRIBUTION LLC, a United States limited liability company, with its principal place of business in the State of Delaware
("Publisher" or "Company")

AND

**AUTHORS:**
1. Dr. Marc Dubois – Trading and Financial Education
2. Sophie Laurent – Personal Development and Productivity
3. Thomas Martin – Entrepreneurship and Startup Strategy

(collectively, the "Authors")

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Authors have created and/or intend to create original digital educational content, including but not limited to ebooks, training materials, guides, and courses (collectively, the "Works"); and

WHEREAS, the Publisher operates online distribution platforms, including but not limited to e-probook.site, and is engaged in the business of publishing and digitally distributing educational content; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish the terms and conditions under which the Authors' Works will be published and distributed by the Publisher;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. ORIGINALITY OF CONTENT

1.1 Each Author hereby confirms and warrants that all content produced and delivered to the Publisher under this Agreement is original work created by such Author.

1.2 Each Author represents and warrants that the content does not infringe, violate, or misappropriate any third-party intellectual property rights, including but not limited to copyrights, trademarks, patents, trade secrets, or privacy rights.

1.3 Each Author guarantees that they have full rights, title, and interest in and to the material they provide, and that such material is free of any encumbrances, liens, or claims of any third party.

1.4 Each Author agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Publisher from any claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any breach of the representations and warranties in this Section 1.

---

## 2. LICENSE AND RIGHTS GRANTED

2.1 **Grant of License.** Each Author hereby grants to Publisher a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-bearing license to exercise the following rights with respect to the Works:

(a) To publish, reproduce, and distribute the Works;
(b) To market, sell, display, and deliver the Works digitally;
(c) To create derivative works, adaptations, and modifications as reasonably necessary for digital distribution;
(d) To sublicense to authorized distribution partners, but solely for digital distribution purposes and subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The license granted herein applies to all existing Works and any future Works created by the Authors during the term of this Agreement.

2.3 The rights granted under this Agreement are specifically limited to digital formats. No rights are granted for physical reproduction or distribution.

---

## 3. DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS

3.1 The Publisher is authorized to distribute the Works through the following channels:

(a) Its own websites and platforms, including but not limited to e-probook.site;
(b) Online digital stores and marketplaces;
(c) Payment platforms and processors;
(d) Educational platforms and learning management systems;
(e) Authorized partner platforms, resellers, and affiliates.

3.2 The Publisher shall have sole discretion to determine pricing, promotional strategies, and distribution channels for the Works.

---

## 4. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PUBLISHER

4.1 COFIBOU DISTRIBUTION LLC shall act in the following capacities:

(a) **Publisher:** Responsible for editorial oversight, content formatting, and publication decisions;
(b) **Digital Distributor:** Responsible for making the Works available through digital channels;
(c) **Platform Operator:** Responsible for hosting, managing, and maintaining the digital distribution infrastructure;
(d) **Marketing Operator:** Responsible for promotional activities and customer acquisition.

4.2 The Publisher shall be responsible for:

(a) Hosting the Works on its platforms;
(b) Formatting and technical preparation of the Works for digital distribution (when agreed upon);
(c) Processing customer payments and transactions;
(d) Delivering digital downloads and access credentials to customers;
(e) Providing customer support related to the Works;
(f) Platform management, security, and uptime maintenance;
(g) Basic promotional and marketing activities.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 **Ownership.** Each Author retains full copyright ownership and moral rights in their respective Works. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a transfer of copyright ownership.

5.2 **Limited License.** The Publisher receives only the commercial exploitation rights expressly granted under this Agreement. All other rights not expressly granted herein are reserved to the Authors.

5.3 **Use of Author Information.** The Publisher may use each Author's name, likeness, biographical information, and professional credentials for reasonable promotional and marketing purposes directly related to the Works, including but not limited to cover designs, marketing materials, and platform listings.

5.4 **Author Attribution.** The Publisher agrees to include appropriate attribution to the Authors in connection with the Works, as reasonably determined by the Publisher.

---

## 6. DIGITAL SALES

6.1 The Works shall be sold exclusively as digital products in the following formats:

(a) Digital downloads in PDF, EPUB, MOBI, or other electronic formats;
(b) Online courses and membership content;
(c) Educational training materials and workbooks;
(d) Video and text bundles, where applicable;
(e) Any other digital format as mutually agreed upon.

6.2 No physical copies or printed materials are contemplated under this Agreement. All sales shall be conducted digitally through electronic delivery.

---

## 7. ROYALTIES AND PAYMENT

7.1 **Royalty Rate.** Each Author shall be entitled to receive fifty percent (50%) of Net Receipts derived from the sale of their respective Works.

7.2 **Net Receipts Definition.** "Net Receipts" means the gross revenue received from sales of the Works, less the following permitted deductions:

(a) Transaction fees charged by payment processors (not to exceed 8% of gross sales);
(b) Taxes collected and remitted to governmental authorities;
(c) Refunds issued to customers;
(d) Chargebacks and disputed transactions.

7.3 **Royalty Split.** The fifty percent (50%) royalty shall be divided equally among the three Authors (approximately 16.67% each) unless otherwise agreed in writing between the Authors.

7.4 **Payment Schedule.** Payments shall be made quarterly, within forty-five (45) days following the end of each calendar quarter. Each payment shall be accompanied by a detailed sales report showing gross receipts, deductions, and calculations for each Author.

7.5 **Minimum Payout.** The minimum threshold for individual Author payouts shall be One Hundred Dollars ($100). Amounts below this threshold shall roll over to the next payment period until the threshold is met.

7.6 **Payment Method.** Payments shall be made via bank transfer, PayPal, or other mutually agreed electronic payment methods.

---

## 8. TERM

8.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of five (5) years from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").

8.2 **Automatic Renewal.** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of two (2) years each (each, a "Renewal Term"), unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

8.3 **Early Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by providing written notice to the breaching party. The breaching party shall have thirty (30) days to cure the breach. If the breach is not cured within such period, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice.

8.4 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days' prior written notice to the other party.

---

(a) Any breach of the Author's representations, warranties, or obligations under this Agreement;

---

## 10. EFFECTS OF TERMINATION

10.1 **Wind-Down Period.** Upon termination of this Agreement:

(a) The Publisher shall cease making new sales of the Works within ninety (90) days of the termination date;
(b) The Publisher may continue to sell existing inventory and access during a period of six (6) months following the termination date (the "Sell-Off Period");
(c) Authors shall continue to receive royalties during the Sell-Off Period for sales made during such period.

10.2 **Reversion of Rights.** Upon the expiration of the Sell-Off Period, all rights granted to the Publisher under this Agreement shall automatically revert to the respective Authors, and the Publisher shall cease all use of the Works.

10.3 **Return of Materials.** Upon request, the Publisher shall return to the Authors or destroy all copies of the Works in its possession, except for archival copies maintained for legal compliance purposes.

---

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11.2 **Dispute Resolution.** Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination, or validity thereof, shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association ("AAA") in Wilmington, Delaware, in accordance with the AAA's Commercial Arbitration Rules.

11.3 **Arbitration Procedure.** The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator mutually agreed upon by the parties. If the parties cannot agree on an arbitrator within thirty (30) days, the arbitrator shall be appointed by the AAA.

11.4 **Award.** The arbitrator's decision and award shall be final and binding on the parties, and judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

11.5 **Costs.** Each party shall bear its own costs and attorneys' fees in connection with any arbitration, except that the parties shall share the fees and expenses of the arbitrator equally.

11.6 **Confidentiality.** The parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of the arbitration proceedings and any award, except as necessary to enforce or challenge the award.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

12.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, representations, warranties, and understandings, whether written or oral.

12.2 **Amendments.** This Agreement may not be amended, modified, or supplemented except by a written instrument signed by both parties.

12.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. The invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable while preserving the parties' original intent.

12.4 **Waiver.** No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right or remedy shall operate as a waiver thereof. Any waiver must be in writing and signed by the waiving party.

12.5 **Notices.** All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed delivered:

(a) Upon personal delivery;
(b) Upon delivery by nationally recognized overnight courier;
(c) Three (3) days after deposit in the United States mail, postage prepaid, certified or registered, return receipt requested;
(d) Upon confirmation of transmission by email (with a copy sent by another method).

Notices shall be sent to the parties at the addresses on file with the Publisher or such other address as a party may designate in writing.

12.6 **Assignment.**

(a) The Publisher may freely assign this Agreement to any successor in interest, including in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.
(b) The Authors may not assign their rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Publisher.

12.7 **Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the parties.

12.8 **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. There are no third-party beneficiaries.

12.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed valid and binding.

12.10 **Force Majeure.** Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement where such failure or delay results from circumstances beyond the reasonable control of that party, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, embargoes, acts of civil or military authorities, fire, floods, epidemics, pandemics, strikes, or failures of third-party telecommunications or power supply.
