## 🔍 TIKTOK SHOP "NEW" vs "USED" DETECTION

### What We Found

| Detection Aspect | Status | Details |
|-----------------|--------|---------|
| **Product Condition** | ⚠️ Partial | TikTok Shop HAS condition fields (New/Used) |
| **Automatic Detection** | ❌ Unclear | No public evidence of automatic "new vs used" AI detection |
| **Price + Condition Combo** | ⚠️ Risk | Selling "new" items at used prices = RED FLAG |
| **Authentication** | ✅ Active | LegitApp checks authenticity, not condition |

### Key Insight: "New" vs "Used" Listing

**How TikTok Shop handles condition:**
- Sellers SELECT condition (New, Used, Refurbished)
- TikTok does NOT automatically detect if listing is inaccurate
- HOWEVER: If buyer requests authentication and item is "new" but actually counterfeit/used = problem
- Price anomaly detection EXISTS but is mainly for pricing fraud, not condition

### The "New" Status Risk

| Scenario | Risk Level | Reason |
|----------|------------|--------|
| Listing "new" but counterfeit | 🔴 HIGH | LegitApp will fail authentication |
| Listing "new" but actually used | 🟡 MEDIUM | Buyer complaint, not automatic ban |
| Listing "used" as "new" | 🟡 MEDIUM | Buyer dispute |
| Generic "new" (no brand) | 🟢 LOW | Harder to verify/authenticate |

## 📊 TIKTOK SHOP 2026 REALITY

### Anti-Counterfeit Systems ACTIVE

| System | Status | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **LegitApp Partnership** | ✅ Active | Official TikTok Shop authentication partner for luxury goods |
| **AI Fraud Detection** | ✅ Active | Since November 2025, AI-powered detection upgraded significantly |
| **Domain Blocking** | ✅ 39,000 blocked | Fake TikTok Shop domains blocked in 2025 |
| **Account Ban Waves** | ✅ Active | Mass account suspensions for counterfeit violations |

### What Triggers Bans (Detailed)

1. **Trademark/Brand Infringement** - HIGH RISK 🔴
   - Using brand names (Chanel, Gucci, LV, Dior) in titles/descriptions
   - "High copy", "replica", "AAA quality", "super perfect" keywords
   - Product images showing brand logos clearly
   - AI image detection can identify brand logos automatically

2. **Authentication Requests** - HIGH RISK 🔴
   - Buyers can request authentication for luxury items
   - LegitApp verifies authenticity (partnership since 2025)
   - Failed verification = automatic refund + ban risk + potential legal action

3. **Price Anomalies** - MEDIUM RISK 🟡
   - Too cheap = suspicious (authenticity questions flagged)
   - Too expensive = no sales, poor conversion
   - AI may flag unrealistic pricing vs market average
   - **NEW (March 2026)**: Fictitious listings rules enforced

4. **Seller Reputation** - CRITICAL 🔴
   - New accounts = higher scrutiny (700K+ sellers removed in 2025)
   - Low ratings = faster ban
   - Account age matters significantly
   - Video content quality affects trust score

5. **Content/Video Detection** - MEDIUM RISK 🟡
   - TikTok's AI analyzes video content
   - Product in video must match listing
   - Brand logos visible in video = potential infringement report
   - Music/sound can trigger content ID claims

## 📋 RECOMMENDATION FOR WAEL - ANSWERING YOUR QUESTIONS

### Question 1: "Will TikTok bots stop us based on price?"

**Answer: PARTIAL RISK** 🟡
- TikTok does have price anomaly detection
- If you sell Chanel bags at €50 (retail €5000), it's an instant red flag
- Mid-range pricing (30-50% of retail) looks more legitimate
- **Recommendation**: Use realistic pricing, not "too good to be true"

### Question 2: "Will TikTok bots detect if items are 'new' vs 'used'?"

**Answer: LIMITED DETECTION** 🟡
- TikTok does NOT automatically detect "new vs used" in images
- HOWEVER: If buyer requests authentication and it's fake = instant ban
- Listing "new" but at used prices = potential review trigger
- **Recommendation**: Be consistent. Either sell as "used/vintage" OR as "new" with proper pricing

### Question 3: "Can we sell products that look like brand X without issues?"

**Answer: HIGH RISK** 🔴
- NO brand names in ANY text (titles, descriptions, tags)
- NO logos in product images or videos
- NO "style", "inspired by", "type", "like" references
- AI can detect logos in images automatically
- **Recommendation**: Sell generic products only, or private label
